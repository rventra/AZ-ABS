0Ground Rules — Read First

Arizona is currently the only state that has eliminated UPL/ownership Rule 5.4 entirely — non-lawyers can own law firms via an ABS licensed by the Arizona Supreme Court. That's the whole thesis.
1099 trap: a "compliance lawyer" doing ongoing, core, supervised legal work looks like a W-2 employee. To keep 1099 defensible: written contractor agreement, lawyer sets own hours, uses own malpractice policy, serves other clients, paid per deliverable/milestone (not salary), no benefits. If the lawyer is functionally running compliance full-time for you, budget for W-2 instead.
Also note: the ABS itself needs an Arizona-licensed Compliance Lawyer named in the application. A 1099 arrangement can work, but the Court expects real authority and access — not a figurehead.
