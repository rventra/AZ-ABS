1Structure & Pre-Application Weeks 1–2

Arizona is the only state that has fully eliminated the Rule 5.4 prohibition on non-lawyer ownership, allowing you to own a law firm and hold a direct fee interest in MDL cases.

2ABS-Compliant Operating Agreement

Do not use standard templates from LegalZoom — they will be automatically rejected by the Supreme Court. There are no official, pre-made templates available on the Arizona Judicial Branch ABS Directory.

3The 1099 Compliance Lawyer Weeks 2–4

You must designate an active, Arizona-licensed attorney with a clean disciplinary record to serve as the firm's regulatory shield. They do not need to be a W-2 employee, but you must actively defend against the "1099 Trap." If the IRS determines they act like a full-time employee, you face severe tax penalties.

4MDL Operations & Filing Counsel

Your non-lawyer staff can act as the engine of the operation, but licensed attorneys must handle the final legal bottlenecks.
